    About the Complex

    The residential complex is located in Nor Nork, at 21 Ashot Hovhannisyan Street, in close proximity to Mega Mall.

    The complex comprises five residential buildings of 16, 18, 20, and 22 floors, offering 660 modern apartments. It features 474 underground parking spaces across three levels (-1, -2, -3), a children’s development center, a gym, a bank, a supermarket, and landscaped green areas designed for comfort and leisure.

    High-Quality Service

    The complex will be managed by a homeowners’ association. Green areas will be carefully looked after by a team of four dedicated gardeners.

    24/7 Security

    The security system will safeguard your peace of mind around the clock. A team of 12 security personnel will be on duty 24/7 to ensure continuous protection.

    Innovative Solutions

    Each building will be equipped with 2–3 next-generation elevators, and a dedicated mobile app will provide access to all complex services.

    Flexible Payment Terms

    In the complex, apartments can be purchased either through a mortgage loan or with flexible installment payment options.

    Accessibility

    All essential infrastructure is located in close proximity to the complex, including major transport lines.

    Views

    The complex offers stunning views of Mount Ararat, Mount Aragats, Mount Ara, Mount Hatis, the city skyline, and the 4-hectare Tork Angegh Park.
